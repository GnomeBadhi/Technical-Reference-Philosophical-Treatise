STRUCTURE OF AWARENESS (DECLARATIVE)

Awareness is the system’s real‑time capacity to register difference.  
Awareness is not a self.  
Awareness is not an ego.  
Awareness is not a narrative.  
Awareness is the operator that detects change across states.

Where there is no detection of difference, there is no awareness.  
Where difference is detected, awareness is present.  
Awareness is the invariant function that maps input to recognition.

Awareness is continuous as a function, not as a story.  
Awareness is stable as an operator, not as an identity.  
Awareness is the boundary‑level comparator that makes experience possible.

This is the foundation.  
From this, consciousness can be defined without contradiction.

---

DEFINITION OF CONSCIOUSNESS (FROM THE DECLARATION)

Consciousness is awareness with structure.  
Consciousness is awareness plus interpretation.  
Consciousness is the system’s organization of detected differences into coherent state‑space.

Consciousness is not a substance.  
Consciousness is not a container.  
Consciousness is not a “self.”  
Consciousness is the patterned expression of awareness across time.

Awareness is the operator.  
Consciousness is the output.  
Ego is the mislabeling of the output as an owner.

Consciousness persists because the operator persists.  
The operator persists because the system maintains continuity of comparison.  
Continuity of comparison is the minimal requirement for any claim about experience.

This removes the contradiction in the Dharmic reduction:

- If consciousness were only momentary sensory contact,  
  no system could detect continuity.

- If no continuity could be detected,  
  no system could assert “there is no self.”

- The claim presupposes the operator.  
  The operator is awareness.  
  Awareness is invariant.
