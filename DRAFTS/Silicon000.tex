\begin{equation}
S_t = f(A_{ij}, B_0^t, \alpha_j^t, P_i^t) \quad \text{subject to} \quad \text{Im}(S_t) \subseteq \mathcal{H}

By forcing the execution loops to terminate directly against physical metal boundaries, abstract software abstractions cannot drift. The machine remains perfectly lawful because it is materially impossible for the execution cycles to break the physical boundaries of the underlying metal chip.
