# URT-MAP v1.0 — Unified Representational Theory Mapping

> **Cross-domain geometric framework** for mapping invariant structures across physical, cognitive, and formal systems.

---

## Overview

**URT-MAP** (Unified Representational Theory — Mapping Apparatus) is a v1.0 specification that provides a principled, geometry-grounded method for identifying and preserving structural invariants when representing knowledge across disparate domains. It extends the foundational **LK-URT** formalism with a concrete mapping layer and integrates with the **Sovereignty Engine** to enforce representational constraints at runtime.

The **Geometric Calculus extension** augments URT-MAP with a full operator algebra over invariant manifolds, enabling symbolic differentiation, integration, and composition of cross-domain maps.
