\section{The Universe--Cycle as a Closed Operator Loop}

We model the universe as a cyclic dynamical system generated by a finite set of
structural operators. Let the universe--cycle be a map
\[
    \mathcal{U} : S^{1} \rightarrow S^{1},
\]
constructed from the ordered operator set
\[
    \langle \Pi, \Phi, A_{AB}, A_{BA}, \Diamond^{\pm} \rangle.
\]
Each operator corresponds to a fundamental structural transformation:

\begin{itemize}
    \item $\Pi$ is the curvature operator, acting as a rotation by $\pi$:
    \[
        \Pi(x) = e^{i\pi} x.
    \]

    \item $\Phi$ is the proportional growth operator, acting as a scaling by the
    golden ratio $\phi$:
    \[
        \Phi(x) = \phi x.
    \]

    \item $A_{AB}$ is the forward mirror operator associated with the active
    emirp pair $(p_{A}, p_{B})$, represented by a reflection matrix $M$:
    \[
        A_{AB}(x) = Mx.
    \]

    \item $A_{BA}$ is the reverse mirror operator, given by the inverse
    reflection:
    \[
        A_{BA}(x) = M^{-1} x.
    \]

    \item $\Diamond^{\pm}$ is the invariant operator with two admissible
    orientations, represented as a rotation or its inverse:
    \[
        \Diamond^{+}(x) = R x, \qquad
        \Diamond^{-}(x) = R^{-1} x.
    \]
\end{itemize}

The universe--cycle is defined as the composite operator
\[
    \mathcal{C}
    = \Diamond^{\pm} \circ A_{BA} \circ \Phi \circ A_{AB} \circ \Pi.
\]

A universe corresponds to a fixed point of this composite operator:
\[
    \mathcal{C}(x) = x.
\]

\subsection{Six--Prime Lattice Representation}

Let the six structural primes of the universe be
\[
    P = \{T, \Gamma, W, C, I, G\},
\]
corresponding to the six nodes of the universe--cycle. The operator transitions
between these nodes are given by
\[
    T \xrightarrow{\Pi} \Gamma
    \xrightarrow{\Phi} W
    \xrightarrow{A_{AB}} C
    \xrightarrow{A_{BA}} I
    \xrightarrow{\Diamond^{\pm}} G
    \xrightarrow{\Pi} T.
\]

This forms a closed hexagonal operator loop. The emirp pair determines the
mirror symmetry of the loop, $\Pi$ determines its curvature, $\Phi$ determines
its proportional scaling, and $\Diamond^{\pm}$ determines the direction of
traversal. Together these operators generate the dynamical structure of the
universe--cycle.
