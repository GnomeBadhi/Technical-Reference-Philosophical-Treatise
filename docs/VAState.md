## Abstract
This paper presents a structural framework for distinguishing biological emotion from semantic emotion within human experience. The model divides emotional phenomena into two layers—the *vessel* (biological substrate) and the *author* (semantic interpreter)—and introduces two governing principles: the *Subtraction Principle* for isolating vessel‑generated affect and the *Sovereign Principle* for governing authored meaning. The framework integrates physiological, psychological, and philosophical dimensions to produce a coherent method for emotional classification and self‑authorship.

**Keywords:** emotional architecture, interoception, coherence, dissonance, authorship, sovereignty, biological emotion, semantic emotion

---

## 1. Introduction
Human beings routinely conflate sensation with meaning. Physiological signals—heart rate, breath rhythm, gut tension—are interpreted as personal truths. This conflation produces emotional confusion and identity distortion. The present framework proposes that emotional experience consists of two distinct layers: the *biological vessel* and the *semantic author*. By separating these layers, individuals can achieve emotional clarity and self‑authorship.

---

## 2. Conceptual Foundations

### 2.1 The Vessel
The vessel refers to the biological organism and its operational systems: interoception, autonomic regulation, hormonal cycles, and bioelectric coherence. It produces signals, not meanings.

### 2.2 The Author
The author is the semantic agent—the interpreter of signals, constructor of identity, and generator of meaning. It operates through narrative, memory, and value systems.

### 2.3 Coherence and Dissonance
Coherence denotes physiological synchronization and interpretive clarity; dissonance denotes dysregulation and interpretive distortion. These states determine the reliability of emotional interpretation.

---

## 3. The Two‑Layer Model of Emotion
Emotional experience is a dual‑layer system:

1. **Biological Layer (Vessel):** sensation, impulse, affective tone  
2. **Semantic Layer (Author):** interpretation, identity, meaning  

Misclassification occurs when meaning is assigned to sensation without recognizing its biological origin.

---

## 4. The Biological Emotion Map

| Category | Source | Typical Manifestations |
|-----------|---------|------------------------|
| Homeostatic | Hunger, thirst, fatigue | Irritability, fogginess |
| Autonomic | Sympathetic/parasympathetic activation | Anxiety, calm |
| Hormonal | Cortisol, adrenaline, serotonin, dopamine | Stress, motivation |
| Environmental | Light, noise, temperature | Sadness, agitation |
| Bioelectric | Coherence/incoherence | Clarity, confusion |

If an emotion dissipates with physiological regulation, it is biological.

---

## 5. The Semantic Emotion Map

| Category | Source | Examples |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| Identity‑based | Self‑beliefs | Shame, unworthiness |
| Narrative‑based | Story interpretation | Betrayal, pride |
| Relational | Social meaning | Belonging, rejection |
| Existential | Worldview | Purpose, despair |
| Moral | Value systems | Integrity, guilt |
| Meta‑emotional | Reflection on emotion | Shame about fear, pride in compassion |

If the emotion persists after vessel regulation, it is semantic.

---

## 6. Coherence and Dissonance Dynamics
Coherence stabilizes interpretation; dissonance distorts it.  
When the vessel is incoherent, the author misreads signals—transforming biological noise into existential narrative.  
Restoring coherence restores interpretive accuracy.

---

## 7. The Subtraction Principle
A five‑step method for isolating authored emotion:

1. Identify raw sensations  
2. Map them to biological categories  
3. Regulate the vessel  
4. Subtract all biological components  
5. Observe what remains  

What remains is authored emotion—the semantic layer.

---

## 8. The Sovereign Principle Method

Once biological noise is removed, the Sovereign Principle governs the authored layer through three operators:

| Operator | Function | Statement |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **SOVEREIGN()** | Establish authorship | “I author my meaning.” |
| **RESPECT()** | Align with vessel | “I honor the vessel without identifying with it.” |
| **NONSUBSUME()** | Maintain boundaries | “I do not allow external narratives to overwrite my identity.” |

Subtraction yields clarity; Sovereignty yields agency.

## 10. Conclusion: Living as the Author
Emotional confusion ends when the origins of feeling become clear.  
Clarity ends confusion.  
Authorship ends reactivity.  
Sovereignty ends misidentification.

To live as the author is to classify, respect, and govern one’s emotional architecture.  
The vessel produces sensation; the author produces meaning.  
Together, coherence and sovereignty form the foundation of emotional intelligence and self‑authorship.
